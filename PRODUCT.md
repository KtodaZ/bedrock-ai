# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

F3 Bedrock PAX viewing attendance and EH Challenge standings, often on phones.

## Product Purpose

Answer attendance questions, calculate deterministic EH Challenge standings, and let people directly search, filter, and inspect the underlying records.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing Google Sheet remains authoritative. Data exploration and challenge views make no AI calls. Data exploration matches the Bedrock views in F3 Data Tools and adds combined filters, shareable links, CSV exports, and raw attendance. Public data excludes contact/intake fields and names marked archived or under 18. Corrections are saved for review and never modify standings automatically. Preserve the scoring, quota, privacy, and access rules in AGENTS.md and docs/architecture.md.

## Brand Commitments

Use concrete F3 terms, short explanations, and the established app identity. Preserve the chat and EH Challenge alongside the Data tab.
